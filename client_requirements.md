# Client Requirements Document: "The Cozy Corner" - Custom Curtains & Sofas

**Project Title:** The Cozy Corner - E-commerce Website

**1. Introduction & Vision**

*   **Project Overview:** We are "The Cozy Corner," a boutique shop specializing in high-quality, custom-made curtains and sofas. We require a professional, modern, and user-friendly e-commerce website to showcase our products, attract new customers, and facilitate online sales.
*   **Business Goals:**
    *   Establish a strong online presence for our brand.
    *   Increase overall sales by 30% in the first year.
    *   Streamline the ordering process for custom items.
    *   Expand our reach to a national customer base.
*   **Target Audience:**
    *   Homeowners and renters (ages 25-60) looking for quality home furnishings.
    *   Interior designers and home stagers.
    *   Customers who value quality craftsmanship and unique designs.

**2. Key Features & Functionality**

*   **Customer-Facing Website:**
    *   **Homepage:** A visually appealing introduction to our brand, featuring best-selling products and current promotions.
    *   **Product Catalog:**
        *   Separate sections for curtains and sofas.
        *   High-quality product images and detailed descriptions.
        *   Advanced filtering options (by price, color, material, style).
        *   A powerful search functionality.
    *   **Product Detail Pages:**
        *   Multiple product images from different angles.
        *   Clear pricing and customization options (e.g., fabric, size, color).
        *   Customer reviews and ratings.
    *   **Shopping Cart & Checkout:**
        *   A simple, secure, and intuitive checkout process.
        *   Integration with major payment gateways (e.g., Stripe, PayPal).
        *   Guest checkout option.
    *   **User Accounts:**
        *   Customer registration and login.
        *   Order history and status tracking.
        *   Saved addresses and payment information.
    *   **"Our Projects" Gallery:** A portfolio to showcase our custom work in real homes.
    *   **Wishlist:** Allow customers to save products for later.
    *   **Direct Communication:**
        *   Click-to-call and WhatsApp integration for instant contact.
        *   Live chat support for real-time customer service.
    *   **Blog/Content Section:** A dedicated area for articles, guides, and news to improve SEO and engage customers.
    *   **Static Pages:** About Us, Contact Us (with a form), Privacy Policy, FAQs.

*   **Internationalization (i18n):**
    *   The website should support multiple languages (e.g., English, Spanish, French).
    *   The ability to display prices in different currencies.

*   **Admin Panel (for internal use):**
    *   **Advanced Dashboard:** A comprehensive overview of sales, orders, website activity, and key performance indicators.
    *   **Product Management:** Easily add, edit, and remove products and their details.
    *   **Category Management:** Organize products into categories and sub-categories.
    *   **Order Management:** View and manage all customer orders, update order statuses, and handle returns.
    *   **Customer Management:** View customer information and order history.
    *   **Project Management:** Upload and manage images for the "Our Projects" gallery.

**3. Non-Functional Requirements**

*   **Design & User Experience:** The website must be visually appealing, easy to navigate, and reflect our brand's aesthetic. It should be fully responsive and work flawlessly on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.
*   **Performance:** All pages should load quickly (ideally under 3 seconds) to provide a smooth user experience.
*   **Security:** All customer data, especially payment information, must be handled with the highest level of security. The website must be compliant with all relevant data protection regulations.
*   **Scalability:** The website should be built to handle future growth in traffic and sales.

**4. Budget & Timeline**

*   We are open to discussing the budget and timeline based on the proposed technical solution and the value it will bring to our business. We are looking for a partner who can deliver a high-quality product that meets our long-term goals.
